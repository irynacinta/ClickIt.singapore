🧬 Introducing SafeSense™

Your shirt keeps watch too.

Every piece of ClickIt cotton is coated in a super-thin layer of carbon nanotubes — thinner than a hair. That turns the whole shirt into a soft, built-in sensor.

It quietly keeps an eye on heart rate and breathing all day long. If something ever seems wrong — like a sudden fall — it lets a caregiver know right away, along with the readings from that exact moment. A little extra peace of mind, woven right in.

Our history

We started at home, with the people we love.

ClickIt began as a simple idea: help older adults get dressed at home, on their own, without waiting for someone else to button a shirt or zip a jacket. We swapped hard-to-use closures for magnets, and it worked.

Soon we saw that the same idea helps more than seniors. Sore joints, weak grip, limited movement, or just tired hands at the end of a long day — anyone who wants dressing to be easier can use ClickIt. So that's who we make it for now: everyone who needs a little less struggle in their day.

🧬 The technology behind ClickIt

Meet SafeSense™

Fabric that senses, watches, and speaks up when it matters — woven quietly into every ClickIt piece.

<1s

Fall-detection response time

24/7

Continuous biometric sensing

BLE

Instant caregiver alert relay

🧵

The coating

A piezoresistive carbon-nanotube layer, bonded directly to the cotton fiber. No chips, no wires — just fabric that happens to sense.

🫀

The sensing

Continuous heart-rate and respiration tracking, drawing on the same photoplethysmography principles used in clinical-grade wearables.

🚨

The detection

A sub-second fall-detection algorithm, trained on real movement data, flags an irregular motion pattern the instant it happens.

📲

The alert

A low-energy Bluetooth (BLE) relay sends the chosen caregiver an alert with live vitals attached — no app fumbling required.

🧺

The durability

The whole system is sealed into the weave rather than sitting on top of it, so it holds up to regular machine washing, wear after wear.

This is a wellness and safety feature meant to give extra peace of mind — it doesn't replace medical devices, medical advice, or a caregiver's judgment. Always follow guidance from a doctor.
